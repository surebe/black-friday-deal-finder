How TrueTag checks a deal

Every listing was read today at the store named on the card, or in a deals source that names the store — the source and check time sit on each card. The verdict is ours and it is blunt: ALL-TIME LOW means trackers confirm the lowest price ever; STRONG DEAL means a deep cut versus the usual price; FAIR PRICE means the saving is real but routine — a few of those are listed on purpose, so you can see we are not just stamping everything "amazing". Prices move fast on Black Friday; if a store page disagrees with a card, the store page wins.
